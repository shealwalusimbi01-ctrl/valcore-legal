Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-09.1

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • No accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no tracking, no location.
  • We do not sell or share your information, because we do not have it.
  • You can back up, export or delete your data at any time from Settings.

1. Who we are

Valcore is made by the developer of Valcore named on its App Store page. Questions about privacy: valcore.support2026@gmail.com.

2. What Valcore keeps on your phone

To work, Valcore saves the following in its own storage on your phone: your date of birth, height, weight, sex (if you give it), activity level and goals; your gym equipment settings; your workouts, sets, notes and training progress; how many days a week you plan to train; your post-workout check-up answers (how worked your muscles felt); body weight entries; your reminder and app settings; and, if you choose, your first name and a profile photo.

Your answer to the health check question is not saved. Valcore only remembers that you saw it.

Some of this is health information. It stays on your phone, protected by your phone's own security. Use a passcode or Face ID to keep it private.

3. What we collect: nothing

We do not collect, receive or store any of your information on our own systems. Valcore has no accounts and no servers that receive your data.

Valcore contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no social media trackers. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts, and it does not use Apple Health.

Valcore uses no cookies. Our website (shealwalusimbi01-ctrl.github.io/valcore-legal) has no cookies, analytics or tracking either. GitHub, which hosts it, may keep basic server logs for security, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy and are not designed to include your workouts, body measurements or notes.

4. Permissions Valcore may ask for

Notifications: only if you turn on reminders or timer alerts. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Photos: only if you add a profile photo. Valcore copies the one photo you choose into its own storage; it cannot see the rest of your library.

You can change these at any time in your iPhone Settings.

5. Backups and exports

When you use "Back up my data" or an export, Valcore creates a file and opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself. Once you send it somewhere, that service's privacy terms apply. We do not receive it unless you send it to us.

Keep backup files somewhere safe: they contain your training and body information.

6. Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything Valcore has stored on your phone. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Because we never had your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children and young people

Valcore is for people aged 16 and over. If someone under 16 enters their date of birth, Valcore stops at the first screen and saves nothing.

For 16 and 17 year olds, Valcore shows no calorie, protein, goal weight or BMI figures, and body weight tracking is off unless they turn it on.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the UK and EU GDPR and US state privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal information. With Valcore you can do all of this yourself in the app, because the information is only on your phone. If you have a question, email us at the address in section 1.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not collect "consumer health data" as defined by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with how we handle a privacy matter, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

In Australia, we follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 as good practice, even though we never hold your information. If you are unhappy with our answer to a privacy question, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Valcore handles information, we will update this policy and show it to you in the app before the change applies to you.
