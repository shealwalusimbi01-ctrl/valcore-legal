Valcore support

Valcore is a free strength-training guide for iPhone. It plans your workouts, suggests weights and keeps everything on your phone.

Get help

Email valcore.support2026@gmail.com. We read every message, and reports about safety are handled first.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. We never receive it. Use Settings > Back up my data to keep a copy.

How do I delete my data? Settings > Delete all data, or delete the app.

Does Valcore cost anything? Valcore has a free plan: your workouts, weights and progress. Valcore Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription, sold by Apple, that unlocks extra features such as customising splits, Recovery and strength insights. The price is shown in the app before you buy. There are no hidden fees.

How do I cancel Pro? In Valcore, go to Settings, Your plan, Manage or cancel subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Valcore. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your rights under consumer law are not affected.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing. In Valcore, go to Settings, Your plan, Restore purchases, while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Does Valcore send me emails? No. Valcore never emails you. Workout reminders come from your phone, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

My gym doesn't have a machine. Open the Split tab, then Your gym's equipment, and tick what is missing. Valcore swaps those exercises for similar ones.

Important

Everything Valcore suggests is a general recommendation, not medical advice or personal coaching. For guidance made for you, work with a qualified personal trainer, and ask a doctor about any health question. Stop if anything hurts.
